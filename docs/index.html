its boundary.

This site records successful checks, inconclusive scans, and detector failures with equal visibility. A missing result is never presented as a clean result.

Performance validation

Three stress levels, four controller variants, confidence intervals, paired tests, effect sizes, and a no-forecast ablation are published with per-seed results. The model is synthetic, not a production array.

AI score 0.133 is probabilistic. Plagiarism is inconclusive without an independent corpus; citation assessment remains inconclusive despite 11 of 15 references verified. The experimental watermark check found no statistical anomaly and did not affect risk.
